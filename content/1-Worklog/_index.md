From **June 15, 2026** to **August 14, 2026**, I followed a structured learning path focused on **AI/ML on AWS**, covering everything from foundational cloud services to advanced MLOps and serverless AI applications. Each week was dedicated to a specific theme, with hands-on labs, workshops, and project work.

The weekly reports below document my objectives, tasks, achievements, and key takeaways for each stage of the program.

---

### Weekly Breakdown

**Week 1:** [AWS Foundations & Machine Learning Essentials](1.1-week1/)  
*(June 15 – June 21)* – Set up AWS environment, explored the AI/ML stack, and began SageMaker immersion.

**Week 2:** [Serverless AI – Lambda & API Integration](1.2-week2/)  
*(June 22 – June 28)* – Built serverless backends, used Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, SAM, and Cognito.

**Week 3:** [Data Analytics & Business Intelligence](1.3-week3/)  
*(June 29 – July 5)* – Worked with Athena, Glue, QuickSight, and advanced PostgreSQL on RDS.

**Week 4:** [AWS AI Services & Security](1.4-week4/)  
*(July 6 – July 12)* – Integrated Rekognition, Comprehend, Translate, Polly, and addressed security/governance.

**Week 5:** [Advanced SageMaker & MLOps](1.5-week5/)  
*(July 13 – July 19)* – Explored Feature Store, Pipelines, Model Registry, Clarify, and Edge Manager.

**Week 6:** [CI/CD & Infrastructure as Code](1.6-week6/)  
*(July 20 – July 26)* – Implemented CodePipeline, CloudFormation, and AWS CDK for automated deployments.

**Week 7:** [Event-Driven Architectures & Workflow Orchestration](1.7-week7/)  
*(July 27 – August 2)* – Mastered EventBridge, Step Functions, SQS, and SNS for reactive AI systems.

**Week 8:** [Final Project & Consolidation](1.8-week8/)  
*(August 3 – August 14)* – Built an end-to-end AI application, integrating all learned services, and delivered final presentation.
