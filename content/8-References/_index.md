### Dataset

The student performance dataset (~3,000 records) used throughout the workshop was synthetically prepared for this project, covering academic and behavioral features (attendance, assignment/exam scores, class participation, homework submission, digital learning hours, extracurricular participation, and behavioral engagement score) with a binary `at_risk` label.
