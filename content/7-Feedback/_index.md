### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The vibe at FCAJ is super friendly and open. Everyone is approachable and happy to help, which made settling in really easy. The office is neat and comfortable, so I could concentrate well. I do think more team outings or casual hangouts would be nice to build even stronger bonds.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
I received excellent guidance throughout the internship. My mentor provided clear instructions, patiently addressed my questions, and encouraged independent problem-solving. The admin team efficiently handled logistics and documentation, allowing me to focus on my tasks without unnecessary distractions

**3. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship significantly enhanced my technical and soft skills, including project management, collaboration, and professional communication. Regular feedback sessions and knowledge-sharing from the mentor offered practical insights that will benefit my future career.

**4. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Everyone here genuinely respects each other. People work hard, but they also know how to keep things light and fun. When things get busy, nobody hesitates to step in and help out, regardless of their role. That teamwork made me feel like I was actually part of the crew, not just an intern passing through.

**5. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible working hours when needed. In addition, having the opportunity to join internal training sessions is a big plus.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
Seeing the complete system work end-to-end for the first time – uploading CSV → Lambda → SageMaker → receiving the email alert. Overcoming the Lambda layer 250 MB limit by pivoting to SageMaker Serverless was also a great learning moment.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
To improve the internship, I recommend updating workshops to reflect the latest AWS Console UI, providing starter project templates (e.g., CDK or SAM).

- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
Yes, but be prepared for self-directed learning. The real-world AWS experience is invaluable.
